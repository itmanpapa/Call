fritzbox_phonebook_synthetic.xml, name_number_synthetic.csv, vcard_synthetic.vcf
    Synthetic data written for these tests. They imitate the formats of
    https://github.com/hvoigt/telefon-spam (no license, so nothing is copied) and
    https://github.com/andreaspreuss/fritzbox_blacklists (GPL-2.0, not copied to
    keep the test data under the project license).
