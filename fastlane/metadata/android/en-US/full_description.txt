CallGuard blocks spam and scam calls and tells you who is calling before you answer. It is a modernized fork of <a href="https://gitlab.com/xynngh/YetAnotherCallBlocker">Yet Another Call Blocker</a> by xynngh, with a new Material 3 interface and additional data sources, especially for Germany.

<b>Features</b>

* <b>Call screening:</b> unwanted calls are rejected before the phone rings (as the "Caller ID app" on Android 10+, as the default phone app on Android 8–9).
* <b>Offline number database:</b> the crowdsourced database of Yet Another Call Blocker with ratings and categories, updated automatically with small delta updates.
* <b>PhoneBlock:</b> the community blocklist of phoneblock.net (needs your own free PhoneBlock account key); you can also report spam numbers to it.
* <b>Bundesnetzagentur:</b> the official list of numbers the German Federal Network Agency took action against, downloaded and updated automatically.
* <b>Blocking rules:</b> number prefixes, hidden and foreign numbers, premium-rate numbers (0900/0137/0180), letting repeated calls through, time schedules. Ready-made rules in one tap.
* <b>Caller-ID card:</b> who is calling, with rating and category, right over the incoming call screen, with "Block" and "Not spam" buttons.
* <b>Your own marks:</b> mark numbers as spam or not spam; your mark takes priority over ratings, lists and rules. Local blacklist with wildcards.
* <b>Statistics:</b> blocked calls per day, reason and source.
* <b>Setup check:</b> shows what keeps the protection from working fully and how to fix it.
* <b>Backup:</b> export and import of settings, blacklist, rules and marks.
* Number lookup, call log with ratings, light and dark theme, 27 languages.

No ads, no trackers, no analytics.

<b>Network services</b>

Some functions use third-party services that are not free software and are not run by this project (Anti-Feature "Non-Free Network Services"):

* updates of the YACB database and detailed online reviews come from the servers of the "Should I Answer?" service;
* PhoneBlock (phoneblock.net) – only if you enable it and enter your account key;
* the Bundesnetzagentur list is downloaded from the bundesnetzagentur.de website.

Online reviews are loaded only when you ask for them (the number is sent); the PhoneBlock online check sends only a hash of the number. Contacts and the call history are never uploaded. Each source can be turned off in "Databases".
